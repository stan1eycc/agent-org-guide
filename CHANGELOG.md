# Changelog

This project uses semantic versions.

- Major: a change that breaks the documented role, workplace, or project-thread model.
- Minor: a compatible design addition or structural improvement.
- Patch: clarification, correction, or non-structural documentation fix.

A version is assigned only when its changes are committed. Future improvements discovered in the private OLYMPUS organization are generalized, checked for private data, and then synced here under a new version. Private names, identifiers, prompts, and operational history stay out of this repository.
